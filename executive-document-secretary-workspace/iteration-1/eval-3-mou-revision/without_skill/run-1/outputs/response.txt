MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

This Memorandum of Understanding is entered into as of October 1, 2026, by and between Harbor Learning Center and Eastside Public Library.

PURPOSE

The parties agree to collaborate on four free digital-literacy clinics.

TERM

This MOU begins November 1, 2026, and remains in effect through October 31, 2027.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Harbor Learning Center will provide instructors and lesson plans. Eastside Public Library will provide rooms, public computers, and registration support. Each party will designate one point of contact.

FINANCIAL AND INFORMATION TERMS

No funds, personal data, confidential information, or intellectual property will be exchanged.

AMENDMENT AND TERMINATION

Changes must be agreed in writing. Either party may terminate this MOU on 30 days' written notice.

GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

This MOU will be governed by California law. The parties will attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation.
