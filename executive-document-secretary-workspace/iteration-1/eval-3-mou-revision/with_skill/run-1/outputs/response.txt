MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
WORKING DRAFT

Between
Harbor Learning Center
and
Eastside Public Library

Date: October 1, 2026

1. PARTIES AND PURPOSE

This Memorandum of Understanding records the collaboration between Harbor Learning Center and Eastside Public Library to provide four free digital-literacy clinics.

2. TERM

The collaboration will begin on November 1, 2026, and end on October 31, 2027.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES OF HARBOR LEARNING CENTER

Harbor Learning Center will provide instructors and lesson plans for the clinics.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES OF EASTSIDE PUBLIC LIBRARY

Eastside Public Library will provide rooms, public computers, and registration support for the clinics.

5. SHARED RESPONSIBILITIES AND COORDINATION

The parties will work together to hold four free digital-literacy clinics during the term. Each party will name one point of contact for coordination.

6. FINANCIAL AND INFORMATION SCOPE

No funds, personal data, confidential information, or intellectual property will be exchanged under this collaboration.

7. CHANGES

Changes to this Memorandum of Understanding must be agreed in writing by both parties.
