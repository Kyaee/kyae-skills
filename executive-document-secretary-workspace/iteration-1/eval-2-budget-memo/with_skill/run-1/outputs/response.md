**To:** Marcus Lee, Chief Financial Officer, Northstar Community Services

**Subject:** Approval Request: USD 7,400 for Records-Management Workshop  
**CC:** Priya Shah, Human Resources Manager

I request approval of **USD 7,400** for a two-day records-management workshop for 20 Operations staff. The workshop will prepare the department to implement Northstar Community Services' 2026 retention policy consistently and on schedule.
